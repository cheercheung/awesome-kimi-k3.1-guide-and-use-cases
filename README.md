## 🍌 Introduction

Source-attributed community demos, workflows and evaluations for Kimi K3.1 and K3.1 Pro, including earlier recordings of the anonymous Kibitz checkpoint.

**Explore concrete tasks, visible outputs and practical limits through their original sources.**

<a id="overview"></a>
## 📊 Overview

125 independent usecases across 6 categories. Reposts and comparisons of the same work are grouped together.

> [!NOTE]
> The Kibitz–Kimi K3.1 Pro mapping remains unconfirmed. Model names, timings, costs and quality judgments are source-reported. Recordings do not establish runnable code, complete interaction or repeatable success.

<a id="quick-start"></a>
## ⚡ Quick Start

1. Choose a task in the Menu.
2. Open its video or image, then follow the source-linked case title.
3. Compare the visible result with the stated task and limitations.

[Open the official Kimi website](https://www.kimi.com/)
